# Bakery and café portfolio

Original fictional studio concepts, not commissioned client work.

| Asset | Subject | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bakery.mp4 | Worth waking up for. | 8 seconds, 720×1280, 30 fps, silent H.264 |
| coffee.mp4 | Your daily good thing. | 8 seconds, 720×1280, 30 fps, silent H.264 |
| cafe.mp4 | Stay a little longer. | 8 seconds, 720×1280, 30 fps, silent H.264 |

Visuals: original images generated with OpenAI’s built-in image-generation tool. Typography: Inter, rendered by HyperFrames. Production: deterministic camera moves and text animation, rendered locally with HyperFrames 0.9.9; web encodes and posters with FFmpeg. No Higgsfield outputs were used. Posters are decoded frames matching each film’s closing message.

Keep the public concept disclosure and per-film studio label when reusing these assets. Replace both the MP4 and JPEG poster together when updating a film.
